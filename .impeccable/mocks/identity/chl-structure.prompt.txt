Create a single premium brand identity presentation board for a computational health research laboratory named exactly “Computational Health Lab”. This is a logo-only exploration, not an application interface, not a dashboard, and not a website.

Concept name: CHL Structure. Design a custom geometric CHL monogram with shared strokes and deliberate negative space. The letters C, H, and L should be discoverable but the overall silhouette must work first as one compact, serious research mark. Avoid a corporate consulting monogram, luxury-fashion serif, university seal, hexagon badge, DNA, medical cross, heartbeat, brain, molecule, spark, shield, or generic AI network. Use a structural grid and make it vector-friendly and recognizable at 16 pixels.

Present the identity system on one calm, warm-white landscape board: one large standalone monogram; one exact horizontal lockup reading “Computational Health Lab”; a compact icon at 16, 32, and 64 pixel simulations; pure black one-color version; reversed white-on-black version; and one very small report-header use. Use a professional contemporary sans-serif wordmark with excellent kerning. Color is mostly near-black and warm white with one muted aubergine accent, but the mark must remain excellent in pure monochrome. Flat vector forms only. No gradients, glossy effects, 3D, mock devices, giant marketing headlines, app screens, UI cards, or decorative background art. Make the board look like a serious identity studio proof sheet with generous whitespace and exact alignment.
