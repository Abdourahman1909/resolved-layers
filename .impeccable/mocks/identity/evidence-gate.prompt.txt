Use a professional contemporary sans-serif wordmark with excellent kerning. Color is mostly near-black and warm white with one muted mineral-green accent, but the mark must remain excellent in pure monochrome. Flat vector forms only. No gradients, glossy effects, 3D, mock devices, giant marketing headlines, app screens, UI cards, or decorative background art. Make the board look like a serious identity studio proof sheet with generous whitespace and exact alignment.
